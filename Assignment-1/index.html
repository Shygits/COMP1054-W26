About the Song and Artist

"Forever and Ever, Amen" is a song realeased in March 1987 on Randy Travis' album "Always & Forever". The song was written by Paul Overstreet and Don Schlitz. The song is said to be inspired by Don Schlitz son doing his prayer and ending it with "Mommy, I love you forever and ever, Amen." It was Randy Travis' first single on the Album and also became his third number one hit on U.S Billboard Hot Country Singles chart. The song was one of three that had gotten him into the Country Music Hall of Fame.

Randy Travis was born Randy Bruce Traywick on May 4th 1959 in Marshville, North Carolina. He has been active in country music, gospel and acting since 1979. Over the length of his career he has released 20 studio albums, and more than 50 singles that had charted on the Billboard Hot Country Songs charts, 16 of those had reached number 1. Randy Travis had reached commercial success in the 80's and 90's but toward the end of the millenium it had dwindled down. Travis had later suffered from a stroke in 2013 leaving him with vocal difficulties and has since retired from music. He has continued to be honoured and win awards and was inducted into the Country Music Hall of Fame in 2016.
